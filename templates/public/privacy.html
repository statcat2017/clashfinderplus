Privacy Notice

What we store

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember:

  • A randomly generated anonymous session ID
  • Which artists you've liked on festival lineups
  • Your feedback on recommendations (e.g. "Good shout", "Not for me")

This data stays on your device and is only sent to our server when you like an artist or submit feedback.

What we store on our server

When you like an artist or submit recommendation feedback, we store:

  • Your anonymous session ID (a random UUID — not linked to your identity)
  • The artists you liked and your feedback
  • The festival you were browsing
  • A timestamp

We do not store your IP address, name, email, or any personal data.

How long we keep it

Raw session data is automatically deleted after 180 days. Aggregated patterns (e.g. "people who like artist A also like artist B") are kept indefinitely without session identifiers.

Lawful basis

We process this data under legitimate interest — to provide personalised festival recommendations. You can opt out at any time by resetting your taste profile.

Your rights

You can reset your taste profile at any time using the button below. This deletes your session data from our server and clears your local storage.

Contact

For questions about this privacy notice, please open an issue at our project repository.
